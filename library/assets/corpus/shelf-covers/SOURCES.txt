TOC alignment (from library/source_toc.json — what each shelf *is*):

- scriptures (extra tile, not in source_toc) — LDS standard works in the reader.
  Visual: canonical scripture / title page. File: scriptures.jpg (KJV 1611 scan from covers/kjv1611.jpg).

- general_conference — Semi-annual General Conference addresses (1971–present); modern prophetic teaching.
  Visual: worship / conference / gathered Saints. File: general_conference.jpg.

- ancient_texts — Enoch, Jubilees, Josephus, ancient Near East & Second Temple context for scripture.
  Visual: ancient manuscript, scroll, clay tablet, or archaeological text — NOT modern books.
  File: ancient_texts.jpg — Unsplash “ancient manuscript / scroll” (photo-1589829085413).

- church_fathers — Ante-Nicene Christian writers (Clement, Ignatius, Justin, Irenaeus, Origen…).
  Visual: patristic era: codex, late antique library, old leather binding.
  File: church_fathers.jpg.

- gutenberg_lds (label: Early LDS Writings) — Project Gutenberg-era LDS books and discourses.
  Visual: 19th-century book room, reading table, historic print.
  File: gutenberg_lds.jpg.

- history_of_church — Official 7-volume History of the Church (Joseph Smith era through Nauvoo).
  Visual: historic Nauvoo/temple/19th-century frontier archive — avoid a flat black placeholder.
  File: history_of_church.jpg.

- joseph_smith_papers — Revelations, letters, journals from the Joseph Smith Papers.
  Visual: handwritten journal, ink, manuscript leaves.
  File: joseph_smith_papers.jpg.

- journal_of_discourses — 26 volumes of transcribed sermons (England, 1854–1886).
  Visual: stacked period volumes, Victorian bookbinding, pulpit-era sermon print.
  File: journal_of_discourses.jpg.

- millennial_star — Longest-running LDS periodical (England, 1840–1970); European mission & doctrine.
  Visual: Victorian periodical / newspaper stack / letterpress — NOT Book of Mormon spine as stand-in.
  File: millennial_star.jpg — interim: same Unsplash “reading room / books” export as gutenberg_lds.jpg until a dedicated Victorian periodical or newsprint image is added (keeps tiles distinct from JD stack).

- pioneer_journals — First-person pioneer journals (e.g. Lucy Mack Smith); migration & frontier faith.
  Visual: handwritten diary, trail journal, leather-bound notebook.
  File: pioneer_journals.jpg.

- times_and_seasons — Nauvoo newspaper (1839–1846); revelations, essays, Restoration news.
  Visual: newspaper stack, newsprint, antebellum print shop.
  File: times_and_seasons.jpg.
